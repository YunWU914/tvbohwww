Frequently asked questions

What engineers and manufacturers usually ask before joining.

What is this website for?

A vertical community focused on industrial equipment and intelligent manufacturing. Manufacturers publish product introductions and technical content for free, while industry professionals discuss openly. Everything is open to search engines — well-structured technical content can be picked up by AI assistants, so when a potential customer asks for equipment selection advice or a supplier recommendation, you may be the one suggested.

Does it cost anything to publish products?

Completely free — product listings, detail pages and technical discussions all cost nothing. The platform does not make money from bidding or paid ranking. The only requirement is content quality: submissions are reviewed, and thin or mass-generated articles are turned down.

How is this different from traditional B2B platforms?

Traditional platforms compete on ad budget — bidding, premium placement and annual fees stacking up, with noisy traffic and unreliable enquiries. Here traffic compounds through content: the more solid and well-structured your technical writing, the longer it keeps working for you, and the people who arrive already know what they need. Manufacturer storefront links are coming next, so customers can reach you directly.

Who should join?

Equipment manufacturers, industrial designers, engineers — and anyone who needs to compare equipment or source suppliers. Small and mid-sized manufacturers with real technical depth are especially welcome: the ones usually drowned out by ad budgets elsewhere.

I'm a designer or engineer, not a manufacturer. Can I join?

Absolutely. Show your designs and proposals, join technical discussions, and connect with manufacturing resources. If you are looking for a design partner or a supplier, this is also where you will find the right people.

What kind of customers does the community bring?

People who arrive through AI answers and active search are usually already comparing options and know their requirement — for example, asking directly which equipment suits a given working condition. That intent is far more precise than generic traffic, and wasted enquiries drop sharply.
